Risk Analysis

RiskLikelihoodImpactMitigation
AI provider quota or model availability4/54/5Multi-provider abstraction, quota monitoring, deterministic fallback outputs.
Unverified or stale source data3/55/5Mandatory citations, source freshness metadata, reviewer approval gates.
Privacy and governance failure3/55/5RBAC, audit logs, data retention controls, AI access governance.
Incumbent bundling pressure4/53/5Differentiate on workflow depth, evidence lineage, and speed.
Low enterprise workflow adoption3/54/5Onboarding analytics, workflow templates, and executive success metrics.

Risk Readout

  • AI security and governance have become buying requirements for enterprise AI systems.
  • The product should surface data provenance, reviewer status, model/provider state, and confidence in the primary UX.
  • Commercial messaging should treat governance as a productivity accelerator: less rework, fewer unsupported claims, and easier executive sign-off.
